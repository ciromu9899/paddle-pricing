Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

1. Overview

This explains how AOKI HIROSHI ("we") handles information for the ClauseLens website and software. Contact: atsuko@shelliecom.com.

2. Your documents stay on your computer

ClauseLens is a locally-run application. Your matters, documents, and library are stored on your own device. The dashboard and matter tracking make no external calls.

3. AI processing

When you run a review or draft, the document text you submit is sent only to the AI provider you configure (Anthropic or OpenAI), using an API key you supply, under that provider's terms. We do not receive or store the contents of your documents. Your API key is entered at runtime and not stored by the Software.

4. Purchases

Payments are processed by Paddle.com or Gumroad, depending on the checkout selected, under that processor's privacy policy. We receive limited order information (such as your email and what you bought) to deliver the product and provide support. We do not receive your full card details.

5. Website

We do not use advertising cookies or website analytics. The sales page loads Paddle.js so that you can open Paddle's secure checkout and also provides links to Gumroad. When you use either checkout, that processor may process device, usage, payment, and contact information under its privacy notice.

6. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to the processing of personal data we hold, and to receive a portable copy. You may also have the right to complain to your local data-protection authority. To make a request, email atsuko@shelliecom.com. We may ask for information needed to verify your identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law.
