"The cases were fabricated. The sanction was not."

In Mata v. Avianca (S.D.N.Y. 2023), a federal judge sanctioned two attorneys under Rule 11 after they filed a brief citing six court decisions that a general-purpose AI had invented. It became a defining cautionary tale of AI in law. ClauseLens reduces that risk by keeping legal research outside the product and instructing its drafting workflows not to supply statutes or case-law citations.

The line we drew on purpose

What ClauseLens will not do — and why that's the point.

Since Mata v. Avianca, fabricated-citation sanctions have spread — from a former Trump attorney's Google Bard filing to a Second Circuit grievance referral in 2024. The pattern is always the same: an AI that will invent an authority rather than admit it doesn't know one. ClauseLens closes that door by design.

✕ No case-law or statute research

It doesn't search or summarize authorities. Legal research stays in the tools you already trust for it.

✕ No citation generation by design

The drafting engine is instructed not to supply statutes or case-law citations. Instructions to an AI model are not a guarantee, so every output still requires attorney review.

✕ No hidden jurisdiction guesses

Anything state-specific is written as an explicit [placeholder] for you to fill — never quietly assumed.

What it does instead: it reads, extracts, flags, drafts, and organizes — with sources pointed to the document in front of you, not to the case reporter. You stay the lawyer. It stays a tool.

Straight answers

Frequently asked.

Does ClauseLens do legal research?

No — deliberately. It doesn't search case law or statutes, and its drafting prompts are designed not to supply legal authorities. Instructions to an AI model are not a guarantee. Keep using trusted research tools for legal research, and have a qualified attorney verify every ClauseLens output before use.

Is this a subscription?

No. ClauseLens is a one-time purchase — Solo $149 or Firm $349. You download the app and own it. Free updates within the v5.x line are included.

Where does my client data go?

Nothing is uploaded to us — ever. The app runs on your own computer, and the dashboard and matter tracking make no external calls. When you run a review or draft, the document text goes only to the AI provider you configure (Anthropic or OpenAI), under your own API account and their terms. Confirm that setup meets your bar's confidentiality rules before using it on client documents.

What do I need to run it?

Python 3.10+ (free from python.org) and an Anthropic or OpenAI API key for live analysis. Setup is a one-time pip install, then launch in your browser — the README walks through it in about five minutes. No key needed to try Demo mode.
